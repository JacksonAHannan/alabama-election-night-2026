## Verified automatic county source

The [first-party civicAPI race inventory](https://civicapi.org/api/v2/race/search?province=AL&startDate=2026-11-03&endDate=2026-11-03&limit=1000) publishes race ID **86901**, which resolves to the [Alabama US House 2 feed](https://civicapi.org/api/v2/race/86901). The payload explicitly identifies country US, state AL, district AL-02, election date November 3, 2026, office House of Representatives and General scope. Both `test_data` and `is_disputed` are false. Its two candidate IDs are 219726 (Shomari Figures, Democratic) and 219727 (Rhett Marques, Republican). Every county has both matching candidate identities and a distinct configured Alabama FIPS. Candidate county sums reconcile to the top-level candidate totals. This observation contains setup zeros, last updated October 30; it does not establish positive-result latency.

The 16 county portions are Barbour, Bullock, Butler, Coffee, Covington, Crenshaw, Dale, Elmore, Geneva, Henry, Houston, Lowndes, Macon, Montgomery, Pike and Russell. Covington and Elmore are partial counties. The county rows belong to the AL-02 contest; do not substitute whole-county statewide totals. The feed has no precinct rows and supplies no write-in counts. Reporting percentages are publisher estimates, not exact precinct counts. [civicAPI](https://civicapi.org/) is an aggregator, not the election authority, and requires attribution.

The [Secretary of State's current general-election ballot index](https://www.sos.alabama.gov/alabama-votes/2026-general-election-sample-ballots) links the county-approved ballots. [Houston's ballot](https://www.sos.alabama.gov/sites/default/files/sample-ballots/2026/gen/Houston-Sample.pdf), together with downloaded Coffee, Elmore, Covington and Lowndes ballots, confirms **Shomari C. Figures** and **Rhett Marques** in the Second Congressional District. Preserve publisher `source_name` “Shomari Figures” while displaying the official ballot identity. Write-ins are a ballot choice even though this county feed does not publish them.

## Reproducible evidence and integration

`research/al02-sources.json` contains the compatible `source_entry`, `ballot_entry`, all 16 county coverage-check records and exact source identities. `.firecrawl/al02-civic-race.json` preserves raw publisher bytes, copied into `tests/fixtures/al02-civic-cd2-2026-setup.json`. The configuration accepts a single source contribution per race/county: a later healthy, verified official precinct feed should supersede that county's fallback, never be added to it.

Raw ordinary HTTP and browser page captures are under `.firecrawl/al02-*`; SHA-256 values and sizes are in `.firecrawl/al02-evidence-manifest.json`. The large-county research retains its own raw download manifest and ballot checks. Earlier search noise for Henry County Tennessee and Pike County Mississippi is explicitly rejected; only Alabama official pages are used above. Prior Firecrawl retrieval was unavailable with HTTP 402, so these checks used public web search, direct HTTP and ordinary headless browser rendering. No login, challenge bypass, external account change or paid service was used.

Acceptance here is limited to source verification and data preparation. Application integration, collector tests, browser behavior and map/benchmark checks are owned by the parent task. All 16 civic county identities/FIPS and both candidate county rosters were checked, and both candidate sums reconciled. No current precinct fixture is supplied because none was verified.
